Control your Drayton Wiser heating and hot water locally over your home network.

Features

- Wiser HeatHub: pair by mDNS discovery or by entering the IP address, and see hub diagnostics and Wi-Fi signal strength.
- Rooms: current temperature, target temperature, humidity from a RoomStat, and the room mode (auto / manual / off).
- Hot water: heating state, mode, next scheduled event, and fixed 30-minute and 60-minute boost buttons with cancel.
- Flow actions: boost a room (target temperature and duration) or boost the hot water cylinder for a chosen number of minutes.
- Dashboard widget: view the full weekly hot water schedule.

Everything runs locally against the HeatHub REST API. No Wiser cloud account is required.

Requirements

- Homey Pro (local platform)
- A Drayton Wiser HeatHub on the same network as Homey
- The hub's local secret

Documentation and source: https://github.com/atwright147/homey-drayton-wiser
